## Help us improve EasyFile Tools

Use the form below to tell us about a malfunction, ask a question or suggest an improvement. Select the tool and describe what happened so the issue can be reproduced.

You **do not need to provide a name or email address**, and we do not accept files or screenshots. Because you are not asked for an email address, **we cannot reply directly** to an anonymous report.

Before sending, remove any personal or confidential information from the description, reproduction steps and error message.
