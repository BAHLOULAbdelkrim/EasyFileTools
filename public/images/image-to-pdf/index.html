How to turn images into a PDF

  1. Select your images and review their order.
  2. Choose page size, orientation and margins.
  3. Create the PDF and download it.

Example use cases

  • Combine several scans into a single PDF file.

Limitations to consider

  • Image-based PDF pages are not automatically searchable text.

Questions and practical tips

How do I prevent awkward page layouts?

Choose the page orientation and margins for your images. Review whether a standard paper size or automatic sizing better fits your document.

Will the PDF contain editable text?

Placing an image in a PDF does not recognize the text inside it. An image-based page remains an image unless it is processed separately with OCR.

Processing and file safety

This tool processes files in your browser. Loading the page, libraries or AI models can still require network connections. Keep a copy of your original files and check the downloaded result before deleting them. Large files may require more memory and processing time than your device can provide.
