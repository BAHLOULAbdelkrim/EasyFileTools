How to extract audio from a video

  1. Select videos that contain an audio track.
  2. Choose an audio output format and review the settings.
  3. Run extraction and download the audio files.

Example use cases

  • Extract a soundtrack from a video interview.

Limitations to consider

  • Audio extraction cannot restore missing dialogue or quality.

Questions and practical tips

Why is there no audio to extract?

A silent video may contain no audio stream, or its audio codec may not be supported by the processing engine. Confirm that the source plays with sound.

Which output should I use?

MP3 is a common choice for listening. WAV or FLAC can be useful for further editing, but cannot restore information already lost in the original soundtrack.

Can extraction avoid re-encoding?

When the source audio codec already matches the requested format and the original sample rate and channel count are selected, the audio stream may be copied directly. Other cases use FFmpeg for conversion.

Processing and file safety

This tool processes files in your browser. Loading the page, libraries or AI models can still require network connections. Keep a copy of your original files and check the downloaded result before deleting them. Large files may require more memory and processing time than your device can provide.
