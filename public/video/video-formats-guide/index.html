Video Formats (Guide)

Compare common video containers, understand the codecs they usually carry, and choose the best format for compatibility, compression, web delivery, editing or archiving.

Quick Choice

Choose a format quickly

Best overall compatibility MP4 + H.264
Best modern web efficiency WebM + VP9 / AV1
Multiple audio & subtitle tracks MKV
Apple / professional editing MOV
Legacy Windows video AVI / WMV
Legacy mobile / low bandwidth 3GP
Broadcast / camera streams TS / M2TS
Container vs codec: MP4, WebM, MKV, MOV and AVI are containers. The container defines how video, audio, subtitles and metadata are packaged, while the codec controls how the video or audio is compressed. Two MP4 files can therefore have very different quality, size and compatibility depending on whether they use H.264, H.265, AV1 or another codec.
Compatibility tip: For maximum compatibility, MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio remains one of the safest choices. WebM with VP9 or AV1 is excellent for modern web delivery, while MKV is better suited to flexible archives with multiple tracks and subtitles.
